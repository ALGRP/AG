## How it works

1. Choose a shared shuttle or a private car.
2. Enter your flight, hotel and number of passengers.
3. You receive a booking code. Our team confirms the details before your trip.

## Shared shuttle or private transfer?

The **shared shuttle** is the budget option: you share a minibus with other travellers going to Alanya at a similar time. A **private transfer** takes only your group, straight to your hotel door.

## Shuttle prices

Shuttle prices are per group, for the whole group, one way.
